# Firebolt sprite

[In-game sprite](../../assets/vfx/flame_bolt.png). Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, then cropped and resized to 512 × 96 with its alpha channel preserved.

Prompt:

> Generate ONE game-ready transparent projectile VFX sprite for No-Art-FPS, a bright flat-shaded low-poly comic FPS. A stylized abstract firebolt flying left to right, in strict side profile: compact sharp ivory-yellow spear tip at right, thick two-tone tangerine and vermilion flame tongues trailing left, 3 separated angular sparks trailing farther left. Wide, low silhouette, about 5:1 width to height, center it with breathing room. Crisp flat cel colors and angular shapes, no smoke, blur, soft glow, gradients, dark outline, typography, gun, character, background, or environment. It must be immediately legible at 40 pixels, on both cream and midnight-blue maps. Genuine alpha transparency throughout the surrounding area and between sparks. Game-ready production sprite, not a concept sheet.
