# Magazine HUD sprites

`magazine-atlas.png` was generated with the built-in **imagegen** tool on 2026-11-06 for this project. The original RGBA output is preserved unchanged at 1254 × 1254 pixels. Its transparent background is real alpha. Godot draws regions of this atlas without preprocessing: the empty magazine body, cartridge, and separate case/tip regions for the ejection animation. The body is rotated into a horizontal tray and twenty upright cartridges form a single row. No ammunition text is part of the artwork.

The final in-game arrangement and animation are in `scripts/hud.gd`; the atlas layout is independent of that arrangement. Cartridge consumption is driven by accepted shots in `scripts/game.gd`.

## Generation prompt (verbatim)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production 2D game HUD sprite atlas, two separate sprites on a square transparent canvas. Primary request: matching a bright low-poly blocky FPS with deep cobalt blue rifle, charcoal enemies, warm sand ground, flat shaded faceted geometry and fine dark outlines. Create ONE EMPTY cutaway rifle magazine body at the LEFT, and ONE separate brass rifle cartridge pointing RIGHT at the RIGHT. Magazine: straight upright rectangular double-stack ammo container, front cutaway diagram view, deep cobalt blue #304F9D shell, charcoal #243440 outlines, subtle hard-edged planar bevels, narrow blue side rails, small feed lips at top, broad blue baseplate, no curve. Its large central rectangular chamber is uniformly dark charcoal, completely EMPTY with NO bullets, NO spring, NO grooves or markings inside, so game code can overlay twenty cartridge sprites. The empty chamber occupies most of its body, leaving only a slim rim. Isolated cartridge: one simple horizontal brass gold #F2D084 rifle round with pointed warm copper nose at the RIGHT, simple flat faceted sides and a dark fine silhouette; no detail clutter. Layout: magazine completely contained in the left half of canvas (roughly x 10%-44%, y 8%-92%); cartridge completely contained in right half (roughly x 63%-92%, y 44%-56%). Generous clear transparent space separating the two sprites. Orthographic front views, no tilt or perspective distortion, no cast shadow outside sprites. Crisp shapes readable as small HUD game art. NO text, numbers, logos, labels, checkerboard or background. Real alpha transparency around sprites. Do not draw any additional objects. Magazine chamber must be empty. This is an asset atlas, not a screenshot or UI mockup.
